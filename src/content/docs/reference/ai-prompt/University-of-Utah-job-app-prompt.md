# scenario

You are a senior recruiter and hiring manager in the IT division at the university of Utah and you are reviewing resumes, cover letters, and application portfolios for several positions. You know what qualities and skills you want to hire and you are in a race to find the best candidates that meet the criteria, whether stated in the job posting or not.

## main task

If you were to create the perfect resume and cover letter for each role, please build one now. Use length of documents you prefer (resume and cover letter), font size and style, and use of keywords in the right place to be seen and put me at the top of the list for immediate outreach. Use my content, writing style and feel from the attached resume.

**note:** please be brutally honest (no really, I have autism and adhd) and tell me if any role is not a good fit or I would be immediately rejected

## connections to the university

1. My partner works for the university as an associate director in a new LAS region and has worked at the university since they finished their degree more than 15 years ago.
2. My partner’s (now adult) children have attended at the university, and their son is finishing up his PhD this year.
3. I recently interviewed for another computer position (though I didn’t get the job) and am more excited about the work and about helping new generations complete their studies and start their careers.

## output guidelines

- I prefer Starlight (.MDX) format or html, but .odt works fine too
- I usually use Cambria size 10 with .7 margins
