# AI Ghostwriting Masterclass

## Overview

### Key Notes

+ AI is not replacing writers. AI Writers are replacing Writers.
+ The market is not saturated
+ You need a **skillset** and willingness to learn
+ **Pledge:** I will not call myself a Freelance Writer

### Good AI Writing

+ clear, objective instructions
- specific

## Technical Details

### 4 Pillars of AI Ghostwriting

#### 1. Positioning

- Ghostwriters = business consultants who know how to write (with AI)
- Solve 1 specific problem, 1 specific person, 1 specific way
- Need your **first** niche, not your **forever** niche
- Criteria:
  - information advantage in that business?
  - industry business want more clients?
  - Are you able to contact people in the industry for clients?
- Prompt:
  - I would like to be a ghostwriting in the industry i will give you and want you to present back to me:
    - A list of 30 even more specific niches where i could find
    - 20 tangentially related industries
    - three bio variations (160 chars or less)

#### 2. Packaging

Premium Ghostwriter
- irresistible offer
- clear, tangible offer stack
- project timeline
- price anchoring
- "easy-to-say-yes-to" packages

8 different ghostwriting services
- big 3: driving traffic: social media, attract leads: educational email course, nurture: weekly newsletter



#### 3. Outreach

- cold outreach (not salesy): Free Consulting Method
	- look at social media profiles
	- write summary of this person
	- look for biggest problem/s they might have
- organic content
- run ads
- wait for referral/word of mouth

#### 4. Fulfillment

build AI prompt with enough details to draft a document or post with only the designated industry, niche, topic, and details to include
